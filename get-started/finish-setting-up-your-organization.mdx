After the setup wizard, Minto opens **Get started**: a short list of what your
organization needs before you can place interns. Each step ticks itself as soon
as the work is done, so you can do them in any order, leave, and come back.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Firm Admin** role. Other roles don't see **Get started**.
- You've finished the setup wizard. See
  [Create your account](/get-started/create-your-account).

## Work through the steps

Every step says why it matters. Its button takes you to the right page with the
form already open.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Get started">
    In the sidebar, select **Get started**. The number next to it, for example
    **1/4**, shows how many of the four main steps are done. **Create your
    organization** is already done: signing up did it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Select **Invite your team**, then select **Invite colleagues**. The step is
    done as soon as one invitation is sent. See
    [Invite your team](/how-to/invite-your-team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add host firms">
    Select **Add the firms you work with**, then select **Add a firm**. The step
    is done when your organization has at least one host firm. See
    [Add a host firm](/how-to/add-a-host-firm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first internship">
    Select **Create your first internship**, then select **New internship**. See
    [Create an internship](/how-to/create-an-internship).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: Add a sending organization">
    Under **Also worth doing**, select **Add a sending organization**, then
    select **Add organization**. See
    [Connect a sending organization](/how-to/connect-a-sending-organization).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: Connect your mailbox">
    Under **Also worth doing**, select **Connect your mailbox**, then select
    **Connect mailbox**. This step only appears on plans that include mailbox
    sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

When the four main steps are done, **Get started** disappears from the sidebar
for good. The two optional steps don't keep it open.

Your new internship then shows its own list of what it still needs before the
interns arrive, on its **Dashboard**.

## Hide the guide

If you'd rather set things up your own way, go to **Get started** and select
**Hide this guide**. It's hidden for you on every device and doesn't come back.
Other Firm Admins in your organization still see it until they hide it too, or
until the steps are done.

## Troubleshooting

**Get started isn't in the sidebar.**
You don't have the **Firm Admin** role, the four main steps are already done, or
you hid the guide.

**A step stays open after a colleague did the work.**
The list refreshes at once when you create something yourself, and otherwise
at most every five minutes. Reload the page to see a colleague's work straight
away.

**Connect your mailbox stays open although a mailbox is connected.**
Only an active connection counts: your own mailbox, or your organization's
shared mailbox. A mailbox that was disconnected or removed doesn't. Reconnect it
under **Settings** > **Mailbox**.
