---
title: "Add interns to an internship"
description: "Put students on an internship's roster, ready to be placed with host firms."
last_reviewed: 2026-07-19
---

Adding an intern puts them on the internship's roster. It doesn't place them
anywhere yet — that's a separate step once you have host firms connected.

Interns aren't created on their own. There's no "new intern" action on the
Interns page; you always add them from inside an internship.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Coordinator** or **Firm Admin** role.
- The internship must have room. An internship with a planned count of 3 stops
  accepting interns at 3.

## Add an intern

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the internship">
    In the sidebar, select **Internships**, then select the internship.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the roster">
    Select **Interns & Firms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start adding">
    Select **Add Intern**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Enter **First Name** and **Last Name**. **Email** is optional.

    As you type, Minto suggests interns you already have. If the student has
    travelled with you before, choose them from the suggestions rather than
    creating a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create New**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The intern appears on the **Interns** tab, and the counter goes up — `Interns
(3 / 3)` means three of three planned places are taken. Their status stays
unassigned until you place them with a host firm.

## Troubleshooting

**Add Intern is greyed out.**
The roster is full — the counter reads `(3 / 3)` or similar. Raise the planned
number of interns on the internship, or remove someone first.

**The same student appears twice.**
They were created fresh instead of picked from the suggestions. Remove the
duplicate from the roster with the **✕** at the end of the row, then add them
again and choose the existing record.
