The Travel module holds when the group arrives and leaves, where they land, and
who meets them. It's what turns an internship with a rough window into one people
can plan around.

Arrival and departure are recorded separately, so a group arriving on a booked
flight and leaving on dates still to be confirmed is a perfectly normal state.

## Before you begin

- Every role can edit travel details.
- The internship needs the Travel module turned on. If **Travel** isn't in the
  internship's section list, add it from **Unused Modules**.

## Record the arrival

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the internship's travel details">
    In the sidebar, select **Internships**, select the internship, then select
    **Travel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date">
    Under **Arrival**, select **Date**, then choose the date and time and select
    **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set where they land">
    Select **Station** and choose the airport, station or terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add the connection number">
    Select **Connection number** and enter the flight, train or coach number.
    It's what anyone meeting the group will actually look up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: arrange a greeter">
    Select **Greeter** if someone from your team is meeting the group, and
    **Transport** if you're arranging a pickup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat for **Departure**. The fields are the same.

## How travel dates affect the rest of Minto

Setting an arrival date does more than fill in a field.

- **The internship's dates become firm.** Until then the internships list shows
  the preliminary window with a `~` in front of it. Once arrival and departure
  are set, those exact dates are used instead.
- **The status can change on its own.** An approved internship whose arrival
  date has passed shows as **Running**, and one whose departure date has passed
  shows as **Finished**. See
  [Internship statuses](/reference/internship-statuses).

That's the reason to record travel as soon as it's booked rather than at the end
— several other screens read from it.

## Statuses

Each of arrival and departure carries its own status: **To do**, **In progress**
or **Done**. Use it to track your own preparation, separately from whether the
dates are known.

## Turning the module off

If an internship doesn't need travel tracked at all, select **Module actions**
on the arrival or departure card and disable it. It moves to **Unused Modules**
and stops counting towards the internship's progress.

## Troubleshooting

**The station you need isn't listed.**
Stations are shared across all of Minto rather than being yours alone. Add the
one you need and it'll be available from then on.

**The internship still shows a `~` before its dates.**
The preliminary window is still being used because arrival or departure has no
date yet. Both need one before the dates read as firm.
