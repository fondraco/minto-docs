Your company profile is what partner organizations see, and it holds two
settings that affect everyone in your organization: the connection code and the
time zone.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Firm Admin** role. Other roles can open the page but can't
  save.

## Edit your details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your company profile">
    In the sidebar, select **Settings**, then select **Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    Edit **Company Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Website** and **Address**,
    then select **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your connection code

**Connection Code** is what partner organizations use to link to you. When a
sending organization asks how to connect, this is what you send them — they
enter it under **Organizations** > **Connect**.

It also works the other way round: to connect to them, you need *their* code.
See [Connect a sending organization](/how-to/connect-a-sending-organization).

## Time zone

Times across Minto are shown in this zone — arrival times, call timestamps,
share link expiry. It overrides each person's device setting, so everyone in
your organization sees the same clock.

Set it to where your office is, not where the interns are travelling to. Plain
calendar dates, like an internship's start and end, don't move; only times do.

## Your logo

Under **Branding**, upload a logo to replace the Minto wordmark in the sidebar.
Your colleagues see it instead of Minto's.

This one depends on your plan. If the section isn't there, custom branding isn't
included — see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions) for the
difference between a plan limit and a role limit.

## Deleting your organization

**Delete Organisation** removes your organization and everything in it —
internships, interns, firms, calls, the lot. You have to type the organization's
name and re-enter your password to confirm. It cancels your subscription
immediately, with no refund for the rest of the period, and can't be undone.

<Warning>
There's no export beforehand. If you need any of the data, get it out first.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

**"Failed to save changes. Please try again."**
Usually this is a permission problem rather than a fault: company settings are
Firm Admin only, and the page opens for everyone but only a Firm Admin can save.
See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

**A partner says your code doesn't work.**
Check they're entering it under **Organizations** > **Connect** rather than
somewhere else, and that they've copied the whole thing. Codes aren't
case-sensitive.
