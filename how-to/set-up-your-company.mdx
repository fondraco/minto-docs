Your company profile is what partner organizations see, and it holds two
settings that affect everyone in your organization: the connection code and the
time zone.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Firm Admin** role. Other roles can open the page but can't
  save.

## Edit your details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your company profile">
    In the sidebar, select **Settings**, then select **Company**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    Edit **Company Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Website** and **Address**,
    then select **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your connection code

**Connection Code** is the six-character code partner organizations use to link
to you. When a sending organization asks how to connect, this is what you send
them — they enter it under **Organizations** > **Connect**.

It also works the other way round: to connect to them, you need *their* code.
See [Connect a sending organization](/how-to/connect-a-sending-organization).

## Time zone

**All dates and times across Minto are shown in this time zone** — arrival
times, call timestamps, share link expiry, everything.

Set it to where your office is, not where the interns are travelling to. Change
it and every date in the product shifts for everyone in your organization, so
it's not something to adjust casually.

## Your logo

Under **Branding**, upload a logo to replace the Minto wordmark in the sidebar.
Your colleagues see it instead of Minto's.

This one depends on your plan. If the section isn't there, custom branding isn't
included — see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions) for the
difference between a plan limit and a role limit.

## Deleting your organization

**Delete Organisation** removes your organization and everything in it —
internships, interns, firms, calls, the lot. It cancels your subscription first
and can't be undone.

<Warning>
There's no export beforehand. If you need any of the data, get it out first.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

**Save Changes does nothing, or you get a permission error.**
Company settings are Firm Admin only. The page opens for everyone, but only a
Firm Admin can save — see
[Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

**A partner says your code doesn't work.**
Check they're entering it under **Organizations** > **Connect** rather than
somewhere else, and that they've copied all six characters. Codes are not
case-sensitive.
