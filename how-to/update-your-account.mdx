Your account settings cover your own details rather than your organization's.
Everyone can change their own; nobody else can change them for you.

## Change your details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account">
    In the sidebar, select **Settings**, then select **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Phone**, then select **Save
    Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add a photo">
    Select **Upload photo**. PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB.

    Your photo appears next to internships you're responsible for and calls
    you've logged, which makes a shared calls list much easier to scan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your password

Under **Sign-in**, enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password**
twice, and select **Update Password**. Minimum eight characters.

## Change your sign-in email

This one takes two steps on purpose, since it changes how you get into Minto.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the change">
    Under **Sign-in**, select **Change** next to your email, enter the new
    address and your current password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm from the new address">
    Minto sends a confirmation link to the **new** address. Open it to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until you open that link, your old address is still the one that signs you in.

## Troubleshooting

**The confirmation email hasn't arrived.**
Check the spam folder of the *new* address, not the old one. If it's genuinely
missing, start the change again — the link expires.

**You've forgotten your password.**
Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. You don't need to be signed in,
and you don't need an administrator — the reset link goes to your own address.

**A colleague needs their email changed.**
They have to do it themselves. Firm Admins can change someone's *roles*, but not
their sign-in details.
