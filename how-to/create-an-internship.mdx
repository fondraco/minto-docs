An internship is one group of interns travelling for one period. Creating it is
the starting point for everything else — you add interns to it, place them with
host firms, and track their travel and programme against it.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Coordinator** or **Firm Admin** role.
- If the interns come from a partner school, connect that sending organization
  first. You can also add it later.
- Know roughly when the interns are travelling. Exact dates aren't required.

## Create the internship

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the internships list">
    In the sidebar, select **Internships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new internship">
    Select **New Internship**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Choose **Individual** for a single intern, or **Group** for several.

    Group is the usual choice. Individual fixes the planned count at 1, so pick
    Group if there's any chance a second intern joins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the sending organization">
    Select the school or agency sending these interns, then select **Next**.

    This step is optional. Leave it empty if you're setting up an internship
    before a partner is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates">
    Choose how firm your dates are:

    - **Exact dates** — travel is booked and the dates are fixed.
    - **Preliminary window** — you know roughly when, not precisely.

    Then pick the start and end, and select **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and finish">
    Give the internship a name and complete the remaining details.

    Keep the name short. The internships list truncates long names at around 28
    characters, so "Prague hospitality" reads better than a full sentence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The internship appears in your list with the status **Requested** and no interns
assigned yet. Preliminary dates show with a `~` in front of them.

## What happens next

Creating the internship doesn't place anybody. From the internship's
**Interns & Firms** section you add interns to the roster and place each one
with a host firm.

The internship also starts with its optional modules switched off — travel,
programme, logistics and so on. Turn on only the ones you need; unused modules
stay hidden rather than cluttering the page.

## Troubleshooting

**"No placement agent branch found for the current user's firm."**
Your account isn't linked to a branch that can own internships. A Firm Admin
needs to check your position under **Settings** > **Employees**.

**The sending organization isn't in the list.**
It hasn't been connected yet. Leave the step empty, connect the organization,
then set it on the internship afterwards.
