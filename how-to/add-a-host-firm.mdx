Host firms are the companies that take your interns. Add a firm once and it
stays available for every future internship, so most of this work happens early
and then pays off repeatedly.

Adding a firm doesn't place anyone with it — it puts the company on your list so
you can call them. Securing an actual place happens on the phone, and gets
recorded as a [recruitment call](/how-to/log-a-recruitment-call).

## Before you begin

- Any role can add a host firm.
- Check the firm isn't already in your list. There's no way to merge duplicate
  firms, so it's much easier to avoid creating one.

## Add the firm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firms list">
    In the sidebar, select **Firms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new firm">
    Select **Create Firm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the company">
    In **Name**, start typing the company's name. Minto searches for real places
    and offers matches as you type.

    Choose a match to fill in the address automatically. If the company doesn't
    appear, keep typing the full name and continue — you can add the address
    afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The firm appears in your firms list and can be chosen when you place an intern.

## Add the details that matter

A firm with only a name is enough to save, but not enough to be useful. Open the
firm and add its **employees** — the people you speak to. They're needed before
you can send the firm a share link.

The firm's **fields of expertise** decide whether it appears under
**Recommended** when you're looking for somewhere to place interns. There's no
control for setting them in the app today, so ask Minto support to record them —
see [How firms get recommended](/reference/firm-recommendations).

## Troubleshooting

**The company doesn't appear in the search.**
The search covers registered places, so small or very new companies are often
missing. Type the full name and select **Create** anyway, then add the address
by editing the firm.

**You already have the firm twice.**
Firms can't be merged, and there's no way to remove one from the app. Pick one
to keep and stop using the other.
