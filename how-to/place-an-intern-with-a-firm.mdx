Placing an intern is the core of the job: deciding which company each student
actually works at. It happens in two stages — first you connect the host firms
taking part in this internship, then you assign each intern to one of them.

## Before you begin

- You need the **Coordinator** or **Firm Admin** role.
- The interns must already be on the internship's roster.
- The host firms must already exist in your **Firms** list. You can't create one
  from inside this flow.

## Connect the host firms

Only firms connected to this internship can receive its interns, so do this
first even if you already know who's going where.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the internship's roster">
    In the sidebar, select **Internships**, select the internship, then select
    **Interns & Firms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a firm">
    Select **Connect Firm**, choose the firm from the list, then select
    **Connect**.

    Repeat for every firm taking interns on this internship.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Firms** tab now shows how many are connected — `Firms (2)`.

## Assign each intern

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assigning view">
    Select **Assigning**.

    Connected firms appear on one side, and any interns still waiting appear
    under **Interns to assign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an intern">
    Select an intern under **Interns to assign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they'll work">
    Select the firm you want them at. If the firm has workplaces set up, select
    the specific workplace instead; otherwise select **Without workplace**.

    The intern moves under that firm immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat and close">
    Assign the remaining interns, then select **Close**. There's no separate
    save — each assignment applies as you make it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Every assigned intern shows **Assigned** and their firm on the **Interns** tab,
and the internships list counts them under **Assigned**.

An internship reading `1/3` under **Assigned** has three interns on the roster
and one of them placed.

## Move or remove a placement

In **Assigning**, select the **✕** next to an intern under a firm to unassign
them. They return to **Interns to assign**, and you can place them elsewhere.

Unassigning doesn't remove them from the internship — they stay on the roster.

## Troubleshooting

**"No firms connected. Connect a firm first."**
The assigning view has nowhere to put anyone. Select **Connect Firm** and add at
least one.

**The firm you want isn't in the Connect Firm list.**
It doesn't exist in your organization yet. Add it first — see
[Add a host firm](/how-to/add-a-host-firm) — then come back.

**An intern isn't in "Interns to assign".**
They're already placed. Look for them under one of the firms.
