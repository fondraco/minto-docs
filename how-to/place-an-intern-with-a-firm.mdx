Placing an intern is the core of the job: deciding which company each student
actually works at. In practice you don't decide it — the host firm does, when
you call and ask.

So placement normally starts on the phone, not in Minto. You call firms until
one agrees to take students, record what they said, and Minto connects them to
the internship for you. Assigning the interns is the last step, not the first.

## The usual path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call firms until someone says yes">
    Log each call as you go — see
    [Log a recruitment call](/how-to/log-a-recruitment-call). When a firm agrees,
    record the outcome **Intern approved** and how many places they'll take.

    Recording places is what matters here: it connects the firm to the
    internship automatically. You don't need to add it by hand afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the interns">
    On the internship, select **Interns & Firms**, then **Assigning**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an intern">
    Select an intern under **Interns to assign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where they'll work">
    Select the firm you want them at. If the firm has workplaces set up, select
    the specific workplace; otherwise select **Without workplace**.

    The intern moves under that firm immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat and close">
    Assign the remaining interns, then select **Close**. There's no separate
    save — each assignment applies as you make it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you begin

- Any role can log calls and assign interns.
- The interns must already be on the internship's roster.
- The firm must exist in your **Firms** list before you can call it — see
  [Add a host firm](/how-to/add-a-host-firm).

## Connecting a firm without a call

**Connect Firm**, on the **Interns & Firms** section, attaches a firm to the
internship directly. It's the shortcut, not the normal route.

Use it when the arrangement already exists and there's no call to log — a firm
you place with every year, or a placement agreed by email months ago. Anything
you connect this way has no call history behind it, so a colleague looking at
the firm later won't see why it's there.

If you're actively recruiting, log the call instead. You get the connection *and*
the record of who agreed to what.

## Result

Every assigned intern shows **Assigned** and their firm on the **Interns** tab,
and the internships list counts them under **Assigned**.

## Move or remove a placement

In **Assigning**, select the **✕** next to an intern under a firm to unassign
them. They return to **Interns to assign**, and you can place them elsewhere.

Unassigning doesn't remove them from the internship — they stay on the roster,
and it doesn't undo the call that secured the place.

## Troubleshooting

**"No firms connected. Connect a firm first."**
No firm has agreed to take anyone yet. Log a call with **Intern approved** and a
number of places, or use **Connect Firm** if the arrangement was made elsewhere.

**You logged an approved call but the firm isn't connected.**
The call recorded the outcome but not a number of places. Only a call with
places above zero connects the firm — edit the call and set how many the firm
agreed to.

**The firm you want isn't in the list when logging a call.**
It doesn't exist in your organization yet. Add it first — see
[Add a host firm](/how-to/add-a-host-firm).

**An intern isn't in "Interns to assign".**
They're already placed. Look for them under one of the firms.
