---
title: "Share interns with a host firm"
description: "Send a firm a private link so they can review candidates and request the ones they want."
last_reviewed: 2026-07-19
---

A share link gives a host firm a private URL where they can look through your
unplaced interns and ask for the ones that suit them. It saves emailing CVs back
and forth, and every request lands in Minto rather than in someone's inbox.

Each recipient at the firm gets their own URL, so you can see who looked at what.

## Before you begin

- Your plan must include **intern share links**. If the option isn't there, it's
  a plan limit, not your role — any Firm Admin can check under **Settings** >
  **Billing**.
- The host firm must already exist, with at least one contact person to send the
  link to.
- The internship needs interns who aren't placed yet. A link with nothing to show
  is no use to the firm.

## Create the share link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the internship">
    In the sidebar, select **Internships**, select the internship, then select
    **Share links**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new link">
    Select **New share link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the firm and who receives it">
    Choose the firm under **Firm**, then choose one or more people under
    **Recipients**. Recipients only become selectable once you've picked a firm.

    Everyone chosen gets their own private URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide which interns they see">
    Choose one:

    - **All currently unassigned** keeps itself up to date. Interns drop off the
      moment they're placed, and newly added unplaced interns appear on their
      own. You don't have to re-share.
    - **Pick specific interns** freezes the list. Only the interns you choose
      stay visible, even after assignments change.

    Prefer the first unless you have a reason to show a fixed shortlist —
    otherwise you'll be re-sending links every time the roster moves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an expiry">
    Choose **7d**, **14d**, or **30d**, or pick a date. The default comes from
    your organization's share link settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add a note">
    Under **Note**, write a message for the recipients. Markdown works, and
    **Preview** shows how it'll look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create share link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Each recipient receives their own URL. From it they can see the interns you
shared and request the ones they want.

## What happens when a firm requests an intern

By default a request arrives as **pending** and nothing changes until someone on
your team acts on it. Open the share link and select **Approve** to assign the
intern to that firm, or decline it.

You can change this under **Settings** > **Share links**:

| Auto-approve | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Off** (default) | Requests stay pending until a colleague approves them. Safer — you keep the final say on every placement. |
| **On** | The intern is assigned to the firm as soon as the recipient submits. Faster for firms you trust, but there's no review step. |

Switching auto-approve on only affects new requests. Anything already pending
stays pending until you handle it.

## Troubleshooting

**"No interns are unassigned right now, so this link would show nothing."**
Every intern on this internship is already placed. Add more interns, or use
**Pick specific interns** if you want to show someone who's already assigned.

**The recipient list is empty.**
The firm has no contact people recorded. Add a contact to the firm first.

**A recipient says their link has stopped working.**
It's expired or been revoked. Expiry is fixed when the link is created —
changing your default expiry later doesn't extend links already sent. Create a
new one.

**You want to stop sharing early.**
Revoke the link. Recipients lose access immediately, and any pending requests
stay in Minto for you to deal with.
