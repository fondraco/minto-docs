A firm's page is the single view of your relationship with a company: what they
do, who you speak to, every call you've made, and every intern who has worked
there.

## Before you begin

- Any role can edit a firm.
- The firm must already exist — see [Add a host firm](/how-to/add-a-host-firm).

## What's on the page

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Contact details, address, fields of expertise, workplaces and contacts. |
| **Calls** | Every recruitment call to this firm, with outcomes and how many places they agreed to. |
| **Interns** | Everyone who has worked there, with dates, internship, workplace and status. |
| **Transports** | Transport arranged with this firm. |
| **Share links** | Candidate links sent to this firm and what they did with them. |

The **Calls** tab is the one to check before phoning. It shows what was said last
time and by whom, which stops two colleagues making the same pitch a week apart.

## Add a contact

Contacts are the people you deal with at the firm. They matter beyond the phone
number: **share link recipients are chosen from a firm's contacts**, so a firm
with none can't be sent candidates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firm">
    In the sidebar, select **Firms**, then select the firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the person">
    Under contacts, select **Add Employee**, fill in at least a name, then
    select **Create**.

    **Email** is worth adding even when you always phone — it's what a share
    link is sent to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a workplace

Workplaces are the separate sites a firm operates — a hotel group with three
properties, a company with two branches. Interns are placed at a workplace
rather than just at the firm, so the intern knows which address to turn up to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the firm">
    Select **Firms**, then the firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the site">
    Select **Add Workplace**, enter a **Name**, then select **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Firms with a single site don't need one — placements can sit directly on the
firm, shown as **Without workplace** when assigning.

## Record what they can host

Under **Overview**, set the firm's **fields of expertise**. This is what makes
the firm appear under **Recommended** when you're looking for somewhere to place
an internship's interns. Without it, the firm only ever turns up if you search
for it by name.

## Troubleshooting

**The firm doesn't appear under Recommended when logging a call.**
Either it has no fields of expertise, or none of them match the internship's. It
may also have been demoted as a cold streak — see
[How firms get recommended](/reference/firm-recommendations).

**You can't send this firm a share link.**
Share links go to a firm's contacts. Add at least one contact with an email
address.
